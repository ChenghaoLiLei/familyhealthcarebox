Add a family member first

In “Family”, add names such as Mum or Dad. Records and to-dos are filed under each person. They don’t need a phone or an account.

Bring documents in

Tap ＋ at the top right: scan (several pages become one PDF), take a photo, choose from Photos or Files, or type. On a family member’s page, ＋ files the document under that person automatically.

Confirm, then archive

The app reads the text on your iPhone only to suggest a category and find the document date. It doesn’t translate or upload anything. Check the person, category and document date, then tap “Confirm and archive”. If no date was found, pick one yourself.

Originals are always there

Open a record to view the original full screen. PDFs can be paged through. After archiving you can still add or remove originals, or “Recognize again”.

To-dos and reminders

Add a to-do any time from “Today”, a family member’s page or a record, and set a reminder if you like. The lock-screen notification only says “A family to-do is due” — no names, no details.

Timeline by document date

The timeline and each person’s page are sorted by the date on the document, grouped by year — not by when you added it.

Backup and moving to a new iPhone

Settings → Backup & Restore: set a backup password and export one encrypted backup file to a place you choose. On a new iPhone, install the app and restore with the same password. If the password is lost, nobody can open the file — not even the developer.

App lock

Turn on the app lock in Settings → Data Security. Opening the app then needs Face ID / Touch ID or your device passcode.

Changing the language

Settings → Language: switch between 24 languages any time. Text recognition also prefers that language.
